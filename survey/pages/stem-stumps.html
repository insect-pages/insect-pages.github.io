Stem stumps

This page collects images of stem stumps created by borer larvae that I encountered in the survey. Among these stump-forming larvae, at least seven different taxonomic families are represented, spread across all four major orders (Lepidoptera, Diptera, Coleoptera, and Hymenoptera).

  • Lepidoptera
    • Hellinsia glenni (Pterophoridae) in Solidago
    • Hellinsia glenni (Pterophoridae) in Symphyotrichum
    • cf. Tortricidae in Rudbeckia hirta
  • Diptera
    • Thrypticus (Dolichopodidae) in Elymus
    • Thrypticus (Dolichopodidae) in Setaria pumila
    • Thrypticus (Dolichopodidae) in Muhlenbergia
  • Coleoptera
    • Oberea tripunctata (Cerambycidae) in Ageratina altissima
    • Mordellidae in Anemone cylindrica / virginiana (thimbleweed)
    • Mordellidae in Symphyotrichum
  • Hymenoptera
    • Caulocampus cf. acericaulis (Tenthredinidae) in Acer saccharum
